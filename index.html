NDS/DS tension ratios.

Why tension balance matters

Wheel strength scales with NDS tension. The NDS bottom spoke buckles first. Equilibrium: T_DS × sin β_DS = T_NDS × sin β_NDS. The 3 mm offset improves the ratio by ~14–15 pp.

Offset rim approximation

Uses Ford's TK540 rim model. The 3 mm offset is applied as: d_DS_eff = d_DS + 3 mm, d_NDS_eff = d_NDS − 3 mm. In a real build, spoke lengths would be recalculated.
